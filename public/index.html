How to play

Real time, real world. The map is the Earth at 1:1 and the clock is the real UTC clock. Voyages take as long as they really take; the crew keeps sailing a set voyage while you are offline, and a paid express passage is the only way to skip ahead. Weather comes from live observations where available, tides rise and fall twice a day, and AIS traffic sails the real shipping lanes.

Survive. Fuel burns with throttle³, load and headwind — bunker at harbour fuel docks (the telemetry shows your range at the current speed). Hull condition decays under way; below 30 % steering goes sluggish and leaks start; at 0 % you take on water and sink. Repairs and a regular service at the yard, or a damage-control kit (K) at sea. Hitting a quay or another ship costs condition; run aground and you lose 5 % per bump. Sink and the nearest lifeboat station comes for you.

Berthing. Harbours have real quays, breakwaters and berths. Come alongside a berth (within 60 m, under 2 kn) and press T to moor, or request tugs within 1.5 km (they cost money but bring you in). The telemetry shows the nearest berth with bearing, distance and depth; the radar marks it. Port dues, berth fees and pilotage are charged like in a real port.

Trade. Take freight, passenger, charter, fishing, supply and tow contracts; every harbour's board is on the chart and in the Job boards tab. Buy low / sell high on markets with real stock levels (buying raises the price), or look around for the black-market contact whose runs pay 5× — with contraband aboard. The ship market sells new and second-hand hulls and buys yours.

The law. Coast-guard cutters patrol chokepoints and hail ships. Heave to (under 2 kn) within 30 s or become wanted. Caught with contraband: heavy fine, impound if you cannot pay, forced reset for repeat offenders. Port authorities also inspect at the quay.

Multiplayer. Everyone sails the same persistent ocean. Close to another ship (about 300 m) or in the same harbour you can trade cargo, form a convoy (escorts halve boarding odds), or board a slow ship and take half its cargo — which makes you wanted. Sunk ships leave salvageable wrecks.

Navigation. Chart (M): drag, wheel or pinch to zoom, tap open water to add numbered waypoints, long-press or right-click to remove one, Sail route hands them to the autopilot (P). Harbours open their job board, ships show their details, the cursor reads depth and tide. Radar (bottom right, N↑/H↑ toggle), telemetry (bottom left), weather & tide panel (Wx). Walk your ship with I: bridge, engine room, cabins; the chart table opens the chart.
