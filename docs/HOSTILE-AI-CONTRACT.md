# Saltline — hostile AI ships: pirates and navy (build contract)

Status: **built** (2026-10-10). Opt-in risk mode; builds on `docs/WORLD-POLITICS-CONTRACT.md` (areas, war cover, `incident()`,
`piracy()`, `stepSea()`, `expressCheck()`) and the existing sink / rescue flow (`Game.sink`, `startRescue`, `finishRescue`).

## 1. Rules in one page

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | **Opt-in.** `p.risk = { on, day, n, last, lastEnc }`, default `on:false`, healed on load (old saves load). With `on:false` no hostile ship ever spawns for that player and nothing else changes. Action `risk_set {on}` flips it (HUD pill and the voyage risk check). |
| R2 | **Where.** Only for an *online* skipper (offline players are never attacked), at sea (not docked / assisted / in the raft, > 4 km from any harbour anchor), inside or within 30 km of a `piracy` area (pirates) or a `war_risk` area of tier ≥ 3 (naval patrol). Spawn rate comes from the dataset: pirates `p100 × PIRACY_K` per hour, navy by tier. |
| R3 | **Caps.** ≤ 3 hostile units per player, ≤ 12 global, ≤ 3 attacks per sim day per player, 20 min between encounters, 2 h after a respawn (rescue landing or forced reset), no spawn while warp > 5× (and warp drops to 1× the moment a hostile appears). All timers run on the sim clock (`game.simTime` / tick `dt`), the RNG is `game.rnd` (injectable). |
| R4 | **Pirates** (`skiff`, 1–3 per group, 32 kn): spawn 3–7 km out (inside the detection range: day 9 km, night 3.5 km, less in bad visibility), prefer slow, heavily laden, unescorted ships, chase, fire small arms inside 500 m (hull −0.1…0.4 points per volley, crew safe), board when alongside and the ship is ≤ 13 kn. They give up against ships ≥ 22 kn, in a storm, after 20 min, when a navy ship is within 7 km or when out-run beyond 1.6× the detection range. |
| R5 | **Boarding outcome** reuses `Politics.piracy()` (`robbery` / `repelled` / `hijack` hold of 12 h, hijack cost unless covered) plus 30 % of the free cargo on a hijack. Armed guards (`pol_guards`, existing) make a repelled boarding 80 % likely and cut the spawn rate to 20 %. |
| R6 | **Defence.** Speed up / evade (helm), `hostile_hose` (fire hose + razor wire: resets boarding progress, 3 uses), `hostile_citadel` (crew locks in: boarding frozen, navy called; pirates break in after 15 min if no navy arrives), `hostile_navy` (VHF call, also triggered by a MAYDAY / DSC distress: friendly patrol answers with the area's `NAVY_PRESENCE` chance and escorts for 15 min), armed guards beforehand, convoy escort (existing halving), comply. |
| R7 | **Surrender / ceasefire** `hostile_surrender`: pirates board at once (robbery or half-weight hijack), navy goes to inspection. `hostile_pay`: release payment (3 % of hull value, min 2,500 cr) ends a pirate attack with no boarding. `hostile_cargo`: hand over 40 % of the free cargo and they leave. |
| R8 | **Navy** (`patrol` 34 kn, `corvette` 26 kn, `frigate` 28 kn, made-up names, no real hulls, no nationality shown): around `war_risk` areas of tier ≥ 3. Ladder: hail on VHF → *clean ship*: shadow, "carry on", leave. *Grounds* (the only basis for force): cargo flagged `contraband`, or cargo whose origin an active `import_ban` / `arms_embargo` measure with `enforce:'block'` of the dataset names, in a tier ≥ 3 area → order to heave to (90 s) → warning shots (no damage) → fire (gun damage by range) if still ignored. A tier-4 area off its recommended corridor with clean cargo gets warnings and a shadow only, **never fire**. Heaving to (≤ 2 kn) or `hostile_surrender` stops the fire and starts the search: contraband / sanctioned cargo seized, fine `max(3,000, 2× value)` capped at the skipper's cash, ship held 2–6 h (`held` as a detention). The dataset has no blockade records; this is the documented basis. |
| R9 | **Friendly navy** (anti-piracy patrol): answers `hostile_navy`, may already be in the area (chance `NAVY_PRESENCE × 0.25` when pirates appear). Pirates flee when it is within 7 km; it then escorts for 15 min (no new pirate spawns during the escort). |
| R10 | **Damage model.** `damageFor(weapon, rangeM, roll, displacement)`: points of `p.cond`, hit chance falls with range, scaled by hull size (`sqrt(3200 / displacement)` in 0.35…1.6). Weapons: `small_arms` 0.1–0.4 (≤ 500 m, every 8 s), `gun_30` 1.5–3.5 (≤ 3.5 km), `gun_76` 3–7 (≤ 6 km), `gun_127` 6–12 (≤ 9 km), `warning` 0. |
| R11 | **Hull floor.** Free/automatic paths (autopilot voyage, express passage, offline, captains, tugs, wear) never go below 1 % (lead's rule). **Exception decided here:** a hostile attack may take a ship under *automatic* sailing (`p.voyage`, assist) to 1 % but never to 0: the crew stops, hands up, the ship is boarded / held (`held`), never sunk. Only a skipper sailing *by hand* under direct fire can reach 0 %: then `Game.sink(p, 'hostile', text)` runs the existing flow immediately (the shells open the hull: no slow flooding), cargo to a salvageable wreck, jobs lost, captain in the raft, `startRescue` (lifeboat < 40 km, SAR helicopter beyond) to the nearest harbour, `finishRescue` gives the 2,000 cr excess coaster. News line: "sunk by pirates" / "sunk by naval gunfire". |
| R12 | **Insurance.** In a `war_risk` area with active war cover, naval hits add repair credits (30 days) and a total loss pays the hull value (same terms as `incident()`), via `Politics.hostileClaim()`. Piracy is not covered by war cover (existing rule). |
| R13 | **Express passage** with risk on: `Hostiles.expressCheck()` rolls the abstract encounter per piracy / war area crossed (existing `piracyChance`, naval inspection only when carrying grounds). Outcomes: robbery, repelled, hijack hold, naval search. Hull never below 1 %, never sunk. Same caps and cooldowns. |
| R14 | **Wire.** Snapshot gets `hostiles: [{id,k,f,lat,lon,hdg,spd,st,ident,name,fire}]` (only when non-empty); `you.risk` and `you.hostile` (banner state, options, demand). Unidentified craft carry `ident:false` and no name (client label "Unknown fast craft"); `ident` flips at 2.5 km by day / 0.9 km at night or when they fire. |
| R15 | **Wording.** Crews are always safe (P5 of the politics contract). Words banned by the politics wording test stay out of the shared dataset and `TEMPLATES`; this feature's own texts say "release payment (ransom)" because the player asked for it, and never describe injuries. Sinking text: "All crew in the raft, rescue launched." |

| R16 | **3D first.** Every dice incident of `politics.stepSea` (robbery, repelled, hijack, projectile, mine, detention) is played as a 3D scene for an *online, hand-sailed* skipper (`Hostiles.present()`, `Politics.run3d()`): boats spawn 3.5-6 km out (radar line, then a VHF call at 2.5 km), approach, circle, come alongside, fire (muzzle flash, tracer, splashes, hit sparks and smoke on our hull) and board (figures on the deck, ladder on the skiff); a patrol craft fires its turning gun (warning splashes or hits) or stops you; a mine is a blast beside the hull. The roll decides the outcome and `fn()` applies it at the end of the scene (`settle()` also applies it if the scene is cut short), so the text toast and the picture always agree. The skipper keeps the helm throughout; the scene needs no risk mode (it is the old dice, shown). Offline, voyage / assist (automatic), express and "no room" paths stay text-only. Navy searches launch a rigid boarding boat (`rib`) with a boarding party; binoculars (banner button) zoom on the nearest contact. Smoke, then fire below 45 % hull, rises from our ship; a sinking ship lists and goes down, the captain floats in the raft and the rescue craft arrives (existing flow). Not built: a helicopter, a boarding party on the walkable interior deck. |

## 2. Notes for the next person

* "Automatic sailing" for R11 means what the server can see: `p.voyage` (set_voyage), a tug assist, a captain, or an offline skipper
  (`isAutoSailed()`). The client's own autopilot sends plain position states, so the server treats that skipper as hand-sailing.
* `hostile_debug {kind: 'pirate' | 'navy'}` (only with `SALTLINE_DEBUG=1`, like `debug_sea`) forces an encounter now, ignoring caps and rates;
  the Playwright screenshots use it.
* `Politics.incident()` and `Politics.piracy('repelled')` now floor the hull at 1 % too (the existing dice incidents could otherwise take a
  ≤ 8 % hull to 0 % on a free path); total losses still go through `sink()` as before.
* Not built: boarding a skiff yourself, several navy ships at once, pirates at night without lights being harder to see in 3D beyond
  the missing nav lights, a Sulu Sea area (the dataset has no such record: add one to `shared/politics/areas.json` and it works).
